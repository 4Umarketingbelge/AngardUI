# Changelog

## 1.5.0

- Level up: a beam of light sweeps across the whole panel, like a glint on a shield. Preview it with `/angard shine`.
- In combat the panel borders turn red; below 50% health a bright glow beats on them in time with the pulse. This works in combat too: the game itself checks whether health is below half, and the addon never sees the number.
- Livelier cardiogram: beats come at the rate shown by the number, so they bunch up as the pulse climbs; each beat varies slightly, the spikes grow taller under load, the line turns orange and then red at a high pulse, and the heart icon jumps on every beat.
- Right click the portrait for the same menu as the default player frame: leave group, loot method and so on.
- Click the name to open the character window, the money row to open the bags, a profession to open the professions book.
- Both effects can be turned off in the General tile.
- Fixed the name vanishing from the card after logging in mid-flight: the last known name is shown and the addon asks again a moment later.

## 1.4.1

- Fixed Russian text showing as empty boxes on non-Russian clients: when Russian is selected, the addon now uses a font with Cyrillic letters from the game files.

## 1.4.0

- Right click a widget square to pick a widget from a list; the mouse wheel cycles through them.
- Widget choice is now saved per character.
- Three new widgets, fifteen in total: Ammo, XP per hour (with time to level), Hearthstone.
- Tooltips: what is raising or calming the pulse, zone climate for temperature, profession names, guild officer rank and location, pet mood.
- Language can be switched in the settings header (same as the game, Russian, English) without reloading the interface.
- The pulse, pressure and temperature simulation only runs while one of those widgets is on the panel.
- Optional panel borders in faction color.
- Experience bar on the character card.
- Brighter heart icon, dropdown arrows in faction color, steadier pet portrait.
- The panel now always catches the mouse, even when locked, so tooltips and menus work.

## 1.3.1

- The card shows the talent tree with the most points as your specialization.

## 1.3.0

- The two squares on the right became swappable, with twelve widgets: pulse, temperature, blood pressure, clock, weather, coordinates, speed, experience, performance, bags, durability, session.
- Pet block: health bar and a row of food that empties as a hunter pet gets hungry. Meat, fruit or fish.
- New Widgets tile in the settings.

## 1.2.0

- Settings window rebuilt as tiles in your faction color: blue for Alliance, red for Horde.
- Larger faction crest on the character card.

## 1.1.0

- Health and resource bars replaced the blood pressure block.
- Option to hide the default player frame.
- Minimap button.
- Surname shown on the card.

## 1.0.0

- First version: character card, portrait, pulse, blood pressure, temperature, guild officers, pet portrait, step counter, time at level, settings window.
